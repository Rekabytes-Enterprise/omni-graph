# Community code of conduct

Omni-graph welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other spaces managed by the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work respectfully; criticize ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections without harassment.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, identities, and privacy.
- Use synthetic examples and protect client and contributor information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and
repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Do not retaliate against someone
for making a good-faith report or helping an investigation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Repository maintainer [@rekabytes](https://github.com/rekabytes) handles moderation.
Use GitHub's report-content tools for abuse. To request a private channel, open
an issue containing only **Private conduct contact requested**, without incident
details or personal information. A maintainer will arrange private follow-up.
If the report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's abuse reporting process.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, give a warning, limit participation,
or block a participant based on severity and repetition. Reports will be handled
with discretion, sharing information only as needed to investigate or respond;
absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. Participants may request
reconsideration through the same private channel.
